Why Paradise

Built around your property.

  1. Made to measure, not off the shelf

    Openings, slopes and pier spacing are rarely standard. Each job is made to suit its site, so panels sit square and gaps stay even from end to end.

  2. Gates, fences and entries that match

    Slat direction, spacing and colour carried through the driveway gate, pedestrian gate and fence panels, so the whole frontage reads as one design.

  3. Glass or aluminium, chosen for the setting

    Frameless glass where the view matters. Slats, blades and pickets where you want privacy, shade or a crisp architectural line. We can talk through the options.

  4. From family homes to multi-storey buildings

    Our work includes residential frontages, decks and pools, as well as façade screens and balcony screens on commercial and apartment buildings.

How it works

From first call to final fit.

A straightforward process, so you know what happens next at every stage.

  1. 01

    Tell us about the job

    Send a few details and photos through the quote form, or give us a call.

  2. 02

    Measure & design

    We look at the site, confirm measurements and talk through style, colour and options.

  3. 03

    Fabricate

    Your gates, panels or balustrades are made to the confirmed measurements.

  4. 04

    Install

    Everything is fitted on site, ready to use.
