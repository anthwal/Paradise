# Paradise Fabrication & Fencing: Website Prototype

A clickable, high-fidelity concept of the proposed website, built for client review.

## How to open it
1. Keep this `prototype` folder together (the page needs its `assets` folder).
2. Double-click **`index.html`**. It opens in Chrome, Edge, Firefox or Safari.
3. No internet connection, installation or server is needed.

To share it, zip the whole `prototype` folder and send the zip.

## What to try
- **Navigation:** Services menu (desktop), the menu button (mobile), and the footer links.
- **Home hero:** hover or tap the photo strips.
- **Services:** open any service. On the Services page, try "What would you like to do?".
- **Projects:** filter the gallery, open any photo, and use ← → or swipe.
- **FAQ:** search, or filter by topic.
- **Request a quote:** the form validates as you type, and you can add site photos. Submitting shows a demonstration message. **Nothing is sent or stored.**
- **Review notes:** the switch at bottom-left (or press **R**) outlines every piece of content as:
  - 🟩 verified from the client's own material
  - 🟦 proposed by us (copy, design, process)
  - 🟨 awaiting the client's confirmation

## Important notes for the presentation
- **Photos are temporary low-resolution copies** taken from the Facebook albums. The final site will use the original full-size photos, which will look considerably sharper.
- Phone, email, address, hours, ABN, licence number, service area, company history and reviews are **placeholders** ("To confirm" / "For client input"). Nothing has been invented.
- The interim "PARADISE" wordmark stands in until a logo is supplied.
- Feedback form: `CLIENT-FEEDBACK.md`.
