## On-page rules
- Title: `{Primary keyword} | Paradise Fabrication & Fencing` (≤ 60 chars). Unique meta descriptions (≤ 155 chars) with a CTA.
- One H1 containing the primary term. Descriptive H2s.
- Image file names and alt text follow `{finish}-{product}-{setting}`, e.g. `black-vertical-blade-sliding-gate-residential.jpg`.
- Canonical URLs on every page. Trailing-slash-free.

## Structured data
- `HomeAndConstructionBusiness` (LocalBusiness subtype) site-wide. Only verified NAP, hours and `areaServed: Gold Coast`. Add `aggregateRating` **only** with the client's permission and if allowed by Google's self-serving review policy (generally not; avoid it).
- `Service` on each service page. `FAQPage` on service FAQs. `BreadcrumbList` on inner pages.

## Internal linking
Service ↔ related services, service → filtered projects, project → service, footer links to all services.

## Technical
`sitemap.ts`, `robots.ts`, Open Graph image per page, 301 redirects from the old site's URLs (**need the old URL list**), Google Business Profile link and NAP consistency (the conflicting addresses must be fixed across Facebook, Oneflare and Google).

## Off-site (recommend to client)
Claim or align the Google Business Profile, request reviews there, and fix the address inconsistency in directories. This matters as much as on-page work.
