# 04 — Brand Guidelines (Website)

## Personality
Precise · Understated · Practical · Proud of the craft. "Architect's eye, tradesman's hands."

## Voice
Direct and confident, never salesy. Short sentences. Name materials and methods specifically. Australian spelling (aluminium, colour, metre).

## Colour tokens
The palette is derived from the client's own powder-coat finishes (charcoal, black, white, aluminium), plus a single warm accent drawn from welding heat and copper.

| Token | Hex | Use |
|---|---|---|
| `ink` | `#16181A` | Primary text, dark sections, footer |
| `graphite` | `#2A2E32` | Dark surfaces, cards on dark |
| `steel` | `#6B7178` | Secondary text (on light only) |
| `alloy` | `#C9CDD1` | Hairlines, borders, slat motif |
| `mist` | `#EEEDEA` | Alternate section background |
| `paper` | `#F8F7F4` | Page background |
| `ember` | `#B4562A` | **Accent:** CTAs, focus, active filters |
| `ember-dark` | `#8F4220` | CTA hover |

Contrast: ink on paper 17.4:1 · steel on paper 4.7:1 · white on ember 4.9:1 (AA for text) · paper on ink for dark sections.

## Typography
- **Display:** *Archivo* (Google Fonts, variable wdth/wght), set semi-condensed (wdth 87), weight 600–700, tight tracking. Its engineered, industrial grotesk character suits fabrication.
- **Body / UI:** *Inter*, 400/500, 16–18 px body, 1.6 line-height.
- **Eyebrows / labels:** Archivo 600, uppercase, 0.14em tracking, 12–13 px.

## Signature motif: "The Slat"
Repeated horizontal lines with even gaps, echoing the client's signature product. Used sparingly:
- Hero image reveal (slats wipe open on load; disabled under reduced motion).
- Section dividers and the eyebrow rule.
- Hover state on project tiles (thin slat overlay).

## Photography rules
- Project photos are the hero. Use full-bleed or large editorial crops.
- Consistent light colour correction only. Never replace skies or fake scenes.
- Every image gets descriptive alt text (what, finish, setting).
