## 4. Customer segments (inferred from the work)

1. **Homeowners renovating or finishing a new build** wanting street-front gates and fencing (the largest share of photos).
2. **Waterfront and pool owners** (canal-front glass pool fencing: Gold Coast canal estates).
3. **Builders and developers**: new-build packages (custom album) and townhouse/duplex balustrades (balustrade-02/09/10).
4. **Commercial and multi-residential**: building façade screens and apartment balcony screens (screens-01…04, 07, 08).

## 5. Brand observations

- The work is consistently **architectural and contemporary**: horizontal and vertical slat rhythms, charcoal/black/white powder-coat finishes and clean rendered pillars.
- Execution standard visible in the photos: neat slat spacing, straight runs, flush gate-to-fence alignment.
- The current marketing presence (Facebook albums) is functional but under-sells the work. Photos are uncaptioned and unstyled, and there is no clear quote pathway.
