# Brand & Visual Direction: "Render & Blade"

## Concept
The client's best work places dark metal blades and slats against white rendered masonry under strong Queensland light. The website takes that exact composition as its identity: warm white "render" surfaces, charcoal "blade" typography and dark sections, with a quiet brass accent.

## Personality
Precise · Understated · Practical · Proud of the details. *Confidence without arrogance.*

## Colour palette (and why)

| Token | Hex | Role | Reasoning |
|---|---|---|---|
| Ink | `#15171A` | Text, dark sections, primary buttons | Matches the black and charcoal powder-coat in the client's photos |
| Graphite | `#24282D` | Dark surfaces | A second dark tone for depth |
| Steel | `#565D65` | Secondary text | Aluminium grey, 6.2:1 contrast on Render |
| Alloy | `#D6D8D9` | Hairlines, borders | Mill-finish aluminium |
| Render | `#F6F4EF` | Page background | Warm white render, softer and more premium than pure white |
| Sand | `#ECE7DD` | Alternate sections | Gold Coast sandstone and pavers seen in the pool photos |
| Brass | `#B48A4F` | Accent: CTAs on dark, numerals, details | A subtle nod to the *Gold* Coast and to architectural hardware. It avoids the "black and orange" fabrication cliché. |
| Brass Deep | `#7D5A2A` | Accent text on light | AA contrast |
| Teal | `#1F4D52` | Swatch only | The deep teal finish on two of the client's gates |

**Why not black and orange?** It is the default for industrial trades and reads as "workshop". This client's work is architectural and residential-premium, and the palette should feel at home on an architect's mood board.

## Typography
- **Archivo** (variable, used semi-condensed) for headings: an engineered grotesk with a fabricated, precise character. Condensed widths echo tall blades.
- **Inter** for body text: highly legible at small sizes, neutral, and it lets the photography lead.
- Both are open-source (SIL OFL) and embedded locally, so they work offline and from a file.

## Signature motif: "the slat"
Fine horizontal line groups appear in eyebrow labels, the CTA band, the service-tile hover overlay and the colour swatches. The hero's **blade gallery** of vertical photo strips opens like a blade gate. The motif is used sparingly.

## Logo
No logo was supplied. The prototype uses an **interim wordmark**: PARADISE / FABRICATION & FENCING with a four-blade mark, the last blade in brass. Replace it with the client's logo if one exists.

## Photography treatment
Natural colour. No filters, no sky replacement, no AI enhancement of content. Large, editorial crops. Every image is captioned with only what is visible.
