## Patterns across the market (from listings)

- **Common:** near-identical product lists, "family-owned", years in business, own factory, phone number.
- **Missing or weak in listings:** a clear service area (only Kelmac states one), lead times (only Oxworks), proof beyond claims (project stories with scope and finish), consistent business details (Next Gen and Paradise both have conflicting addresses).
- **Weak conversion signals:** generic "contact us" calls to action, no explanation of what happens after an enquiry, catalogue-style service lists that make the customer self-diagnose.

## 12 evidence-based improvement opportunities

| # | Opportunity | Evidence | Implemented in prototype |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Lead with real work, not claims.** The hero is made of the client's own photos. | Competitors rely on "years" and "family-owned" claims. NN/g and Houzz show the role of portfolio imagery. | Hero blade gallery and project stories |
| 2 | **Sell the whole frontage.** Gate, entry gate and fence designed as one. | The client's Custom Projects album. Not positioned this way in competitor listings. | Home "Whole frontages" section, Custom Fabrication page |
| 3 | **Show commercial capability with photos.** | Façade and balcony screen photos in the client's albums | Commercial façade project story, "homes to multi-storey" reason |
| 4 | **Goal-based navigation** ("What would you like to do?") | Product-list navigation makes customers self-diagnose | Services chooser |
| 5 | **Project stories with scope, style and finish** | Galleries without context are the norm | Projects page stories |
| 6 | **Colour guidance from real work** | Colour is a common question. No evidence competitors show it. | Finishes section |
| 7 | **Explain what happens next** | Hesitation research. Oxworks' lead-time transparency. | Process section, contact sidebar |
| 8 | **Photo upload in the quote form** | Reduces back-and-forth and supports accurate quotes | Contact form dropzone |
| 9 | **Short form (5 required fields)** | Baymard field-count research | Contact form |
| 10 | **Licence number prominent** | QBCC advertising rule. Total Vision shows it. | Footer placeholder (needs client number) |
| 11 | **Clear service area** | Only one competitor states it | Service area section (needs client input) |
| 12 | **Verified reviews only, linked to source** | ACCC guidance on fake or curated reviews | Review slots plus Facebook link, no invented testimonials |
