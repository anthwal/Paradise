## Market patterns

- The category is crowded with **near-identical product lists**: gates, pool fencing, balustrades and screens.
- Most players present as **catalogues and directories**, not portfolios. Photography is rarely the hero.
- Trust signals used: years trading, QBCC licence, "family-owned", workmanship guarantee.
- Automation (motorised sliding gates) is standard in the gate segment.

## Differentiation strategy for Paradise

1. **Portfolio-first architectural positioning.** "Made-to-measure metalwork for modern Gold Coast homes." Let the real project photos sell. No competitor found leads with editorial project storytelling.
2. **Design-coordinated front-of-house packages.** The Custom Projects album shows matching gate, pedestrian gate and fence infills. Sell the *whole frontage*, not single items.
3. **Residential and commercial range.** Commercial façade and apartment screens are rare in this competitor set's messaging. A dedicated "Builders & Commercial" pathway captures B2B enquiries.
4. **Fabricated locally, installed by the same team** (confirm the factory and in-house install).
5. **Frictionless quoting:** short form, photo upload (phase 2), click-to-call and sticky mobile CTA.
