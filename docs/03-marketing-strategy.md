# 03 — Marketing & Website Strategy

## Objective
Generate qualified quote requests (form, phone, email) from Gold Coast homeowners, builders and commercial clients.

## Positioning statement
For Gold Coast homeowners and builders who want gates, fencing, screens and balustrades that look *designed*, not off-the-shelf, Paradise Fabrication & Fencing makes custom aluminium and glass work to measure and installs it to a clean architectural finish. *(Add "from our own Southport factory" and the years claim once confirmed.)*

## Audiences and what they need to see

| Segment | Trigger | Key question | Proof on site | Primary path |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Homeowner (new build / reno) | Finishing the street frontage | "Will it match my house, and what will it cost?" | Front-of-house project stories, finishes, process | Home → Gates / Slat fencing → Quote |
| Pool owner | New pool or compliance | "Is it compliant, and does it look good?" | Glass and aluminium options, pool FAQs | Pool Fencing → Quote |
| Builder / developer | Package for multiple dwellings | "Can they deliver to schedule and spec?" | Townhouse balustrades, multi-res screens | Builders & Commercial section → Contact |
| Commercial / strata | Façade or balcony screens | "Have they done buildings like ours?" | Commercial screen projects | Screens & Awnings → Quote |

## Messaging pillars
1. **Made to measure:** every job is fabricated to the site.
2. **Architectural finish:** straight lines, even spacing, matched colours.
3. **One team, start to finish:** measure, fabricate and install *(confirm in-house install)*.
4. **Residential to commercial:** proven on homes and multi-storey buildings.

## Conversion strategy (CRO)
- **Primary CTA:** "Request a Quote" (header, hero, every service page, footer, sticky mobile bar).
- **Secondary CTA:** "View Our Projects".
- **Mobile:** sticky bottom bar with Call and Quote.
- **Form:** 5 fields (name, phone, email, service [select], suburb) plus an optional message. Honeypot plus timing check, with no CAPTCHA friction.
- **Contextual CTAs:** service pages pre-select the service in the form (`/contact?service=pool-fencing`).
- **Trust signals, only when confirmed:** QBCC licence, family-owned, years, Facebook rating, factory.
- **Tracked events:** `generate_lead` (form success), `click_phone`, `click_email`, `cta_quote_click` (with location), `project_filter`.

## Content principles
- Real photography only. No stock.
- Plain Australian English, short sentences, practical detail.
- No invented testimonials, numbers, warranties or certifications.
